Delete Your Account

Shriuday Garments mobile app | Last updated: 8 October 2026

You can ask us to delete your Shriuday Garments account and the personal data linked to it at any time.

How to request deletion

  • Send an email to shreyansgolchha433@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account".
  • Write the registered phone number (and GST number, if you have one) of the account you want deleted, so we can identify it.
  • Send the email from your registered email address, or tell us your registered phone number so we can verify the request.

What happens next

  • We will confirm your request and process it within a reasonable time.
  • Your account will be removed and your login will stop working.
  • Your personal details (name, phone numbers, email, delivery address and referral details) will be deleted or anonymised.

What we may keep

We may need to keep some records after your account is deleted, such as invoices, order and payment records, and any unpaid balance, because accounting, tax and legal rules require it. These are kept only for as long as needed for those purposes.
