Privacy Policy

Shriuday Garments mobile app | Last updated: 8 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Shriuday Garments app ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. The App is a wholesale ordering app for registered garment customers and brokers. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

When you create an account or use the App we collect:

  • Account details: full name, phone number(s), optional email address, and your password (stored in encrypted/hashed form on our server).
  • Business details: GST number, delivery address, and customer type (for example wholesaler, semi-wholesaler or retailer).
  • Referral details: the name and phone number of a broker or referrer, if you enter them.
  • Broker accounts: a phone number and a PIN used to sign brokers in.
  • Order and account activity: items you order, order history, credit limit, credit used, payments, and statements shown to you in the App.
  • Technical data kept on your phone: a login session token and your shopping cart, saved locally so you stay signed in.

We do not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and verify your account, and to approve credit where applicable.
  • To process, deliver and invoice your orders.
  • To show your order history, credit balance and account statements.
  • To contact you about your account or orders.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the App secure.

3. Payments

Online payments are processed by Razorpay. Your card, UPI or bank details are entered on Razorpay's secure screen and are handled by Razorpay under its own privacy policy. We receive only the payment result (such as payment ID and status) and do not store your card or bank credentials.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us run the App, such as Razorpay (payments) and the cloud hosting, database and image-hosting services we use.
  • Our own staff and your assigned broker or sales representative, where needed to handle your account and orders.
  • Authorities, if required by law.

5. Data storage and security

Your information is stored on secure cloud servers and transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. We use reasonable technical measures to protect it, but no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your account and order records for as long as your account is active and as required for accounting, tax and legal purposes. Some records, such as invoices and payment records, may need to be retained even after an account is deleted.

7. Your choices and account deletion

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information at any time. To request account deletion or data removal, email us at shreyansgolchha433@gmail.com from your registered phone number or email, with the subject "Delete my account". We will respond within a reasonable time and delete or anonymise your data, except records we must legally retain.

8. Children

The App is intended for business customers and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version. Continued use of the App means you accept the updated policy.
